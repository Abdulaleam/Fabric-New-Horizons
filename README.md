# New Horizons

**A chaotic survival overhaul that completely changes early-game Minecraft progression.**

Forget getting iron on day one or instantly turning trees into planks. This mod slows progression down with strange crafting systems, cursed mechanics, and questionable science.

Built for players who want survival to feel fresh, longer, and much more chaotic with friends.

# Iron Progression

Iron can no longer be obtained normally.

To create iron ingots, you must go through a full reduction process:

Boil water bottles to obtain Hydrogen Gas
Mine iron ore to receive Oxidized Crushed Iron
Convert it into Oxidized Iron Dust
Combine the dust with Hydrogen to create Reduced Iron
Smelt Reduced Iron into Iron Ingots

You may also attempt “friendly interactions” with iron golems.

# Wood Progression

Trees no longer drop usable wood directly.

Instead:

Punch logs to collect Tree Bark
Craft  Tree Bark into Tree Fibers.(You can use the Woodizer machine to turn barks into fibers)
Cover Tree Fibers Into Planks using the Presser machine.

Early-game wood gathering is intentionally slower and more survival-focused.

# Cobblestone Progression

Mining stone no longer gives cobblestone.

Stone drops Stone Dust
4 Stone Dust crafts into Cobblestone

# The Magic Nyaa Tool

Mining amethyst blocks may reward you with the mysterious Magic Nyaa Tool.

This tool can transform almost anything into biscuits.

Nobody understands why, its just magic my man.

Food Effects
Cooked Baskota grants powerful positive effects
Burnt Baskota damages and poisons the player
(although it somehow still tastes good).

# Samir Block

Be careful around the Samir Block.

It flies.
It launches players.
It causes problems.

What This Mod Is About

New Horizons is designed to make Minecraft progression:

Longer
More experimental
More difficult
More ridiculous

The mod mixes survival gameplay with intentionally cursed mechanics to create a unique progression experience.

Recommended Experience

Playing with friends is highly recommended.

The suffering becomes much funnier that way.
